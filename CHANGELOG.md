## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-14

### Added
- `DESIGN.md`: core rules with MUST / SHOULD / MAY levels and rule IDs, covering principles, design tokens, color, typography, layout, responsive behavior, components and states, interaction, navigation, content, accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA), prototyping, and AI-assisted generation.
- Neutral fallback tokens with measured contrast ratios.
- `brandbook/`: guide, fill-in template, and assets folder.
- `docs/`: prototyping workflow, validation (checklist, testing, scored audits), and customization.
- `templates/`: audit scorecard and exception log.
- `examples/`: a fictional brand book worked through end to end.
- `AGENTS.md` for AI assistants.
